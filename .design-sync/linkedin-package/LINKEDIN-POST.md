I built a personal productivity studio around one simple idea:

Your attention is your most valuable asset.

MAKE IT COUNT brings focus, planning, habits, goals, learning, analytics, and short mental-reset games into one visual workspace.

What I focused on while building it:

- A cinematic interface with purposeful motion
- Dark and warm-ivory light themes
- Focus modes for different kinds of work
- Scheduling, tasks, habits, goals, and learning in one system
- Progress analytics, rewards, and small challenges
- A public, read-only demo that starts at zero and never exposes personal data

The project is still in beta, and I am continuing to refine the experience, performance, and interactions.

Try the public demo: https://karthiklifehq.site/demo

I would love honest feedback: which part would you improve next?
